Questions for Chief Operations Officer:
1. What does leadership mean to you, and how would you guide the Operations team in executing CNCP events?
2. Share a time you led or organized something (an event, project, or group task). What was your role and how did it go?

Questions for Vice Chief Operations Officer:
1. How would you support the Chief Operations Officer in managing and coordinating the team?
2. How would you handle a disagreement or conflict within the team during an event?

Questions for Chief Creatives Officer:
1. How would you inspire and lead a team of creatives to produce their best work?
2. Describe a time you took the lead on a creative project or task. What did you learn from it?

Questions for Vice Chief Creatives Officer:
1. How would you support the Chief Creatives Officer in keeping projects on track and organized?
2. How would you handle it when team members disagree on a design or creative direction?

Questions for Chief Marketing Officer:
1. What does leading a marketing team look like to you, and how would you set the direction for CNCP's brand?
2. Tell us about a time you led or took charge of a project or activity from start to finish.

Questions for Vice Chief Marketing Officer:
1. How would you help the Chief Marketing Officer plan and pace the team's content and campaigns?
2. If two team members had different ideas for a campaign, how would you help them move forward?

Questions for Chief Relations Officer:
1. How would you lead the Relations team in building partnerships and keeping members engaged?
2. Describe a time you represented a group or helped connect people to work together. How did it go?

Questions for Vice Chief Relations Officer:
1. How would you support the Chief Relations Officer in coordinating the team's activities?
2. How would you handle feedback or concerns from members about the organization?

Questions for Chief Administrative Officer:
1. How would you lead the Administrative team in keeping the organization's records and documents reliable?
2. Describe a time you managed something carefully, like documents, schedules, or records. How did you stay organized?

Questions for Vice Chief Administrative Officer:
1. How would you support the Chief Administrative Officer in managing day-to-day tasks and deadlines?
2. How would you handle confidential or sensitive information responsibly?

Questions for Vice Chief Executive Officer:
1. What does being part of the organization's executive team mean to you, and how would you contribute as Vice Chief Executive Officer?
2. Describe a time you supported or stepped in for a leader. What did you do, and what did you learn from it?
